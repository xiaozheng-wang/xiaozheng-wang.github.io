#### Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of human–computer interaction, extended reality (XR), and machine learning. I study how human motion and interaction data capture individual behavior, with a focus on motion-based user identification, full-body motion reconstruction, and generalization across tasks.

I also develop and evaluate synthetic XR motion data to expand behavioral datasets and support reproducible research. My work examines the realism, utility, and privacy implications of these data, including whether synthesized trajectories preserve meaningful behavioral characteristics or reveal information about their source participants.
